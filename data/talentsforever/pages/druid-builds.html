Druid builds by role: cat, bear, Moonkin and healing

Feral Combat holds the Cat Form and Bear Form talents. Of the 12,299 builds with 41 or more points planned here since the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685 that lead in Feral Combat, 33% have a point in Natural Reaction (more dodge and a chance at 5 Rage when you dodge), 54% in Lacerate (lacerates the enemy target, making them bleed for 20 damage over 15 sec plus 10% weapon damage per existing application of Lacerate on the target), 55% in Primal Bite (generates a high amount of threat), 67% in Predatory Discipline (your Ferocious Bite ability no longer converts extra Energy into damage, but still deals extra damage based on your excess Energy) and 48% in Shifting Power (turns Mana into 40 Energy, the Cat Form resource). Balance is the Moonkin, or boomkin, tree: Moonkin Form is its row 7 talent, and 3,890 builds with 41 or more points lead there. Restoration is the healing, or resto, tree: Wild Growth (heals the target and their party) is its row 7 talent, and 5,046 builds with 41 or more points lead there.

How this list is counted the windows, the weights, when it starts again

Builds within a few points of each other count as one family, shown by its most used member. The rows and the spec split come from the 21,235 Druid builds with 41 or more points planned here since the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685, ranked by what people did with each family since then: a share or a save weighs 5, a link open 3, making the build 2 and a view 1. The shared, saved and opened counts run from 13 Sep, before the beta: a row's share is its family's shares, saves and link opens over every Druid build's (6,463 shares, 14,081 saves, 133,746 link opens since 13 Sep), so a row can hold a larger share than one ranked above it.

Since 13 Sep, 105,025 Druid builds were made, shared, saved or opened here and 21,305 of them have all 51 points, those today's trees no longer allow included; this list counts the 21,235 with 41 or more points that today's trees allow, since the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685.

This is a count of what people plan on this site, not a tier list. When a patch or hotfix moves, adds, removes or renames a Druid talent or changes what it needs first, the count starts again from the day after it and shows once 1,000 builds with 41 or more points are made on the new trees, as it did after the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685.
