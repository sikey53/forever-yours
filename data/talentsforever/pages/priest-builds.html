Priest builds at level 30, the beta's cap 21 points

The beta cap rose to level 30 on 1 Oct, so 21 points. Between 1 Oct and 4 Nov 2026, 329 people-days went to 21-point Priest builds set at level 30, and no build family has reached the floors yet (builds within four points count as one, and a family is shown once it has five or more people-days and its most-made build was made, shared or saved by at least two people on one day and three people-days in all).

No Discipline 21-point build at level 30 has reached the floor yet (183 people-days in the window), though Discipline leads the leveling orders, a different count from level 60: 59,452 of the 137,799 Priest leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 (43.1%). The Discipline leveling order's first 21 points are Twin Disciplines 5/5, Mental Strength 5/5, Improved Power Word: Shield 3/3, Meditation 3/3, Inner Focus, Soul Warding and Mental Agility 3/3. At each step it is the path the most builds hold: 4,399 of 37,279 Discipline builds hold its levels 10 to 19 and 976 of 6,134 hold its levels 20 to 29. How this list is counted the windows, the weights, when it starts again

Builds within a few points of each other count as one family, shown by its most used member. The rows and the spec split come from the 13,171 Priest builds with 41 or more points planned here since the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685, ranked by what people did with each family since then: a share or a save weighs 5, a link open 3, making the build 2 and a view 1. The shared, saved and opened counts run from 13 Sep, before the beta: a row's share is its family's shares, saves and link opens over every Priest build's (4,953 shares, 11,454 saves, 113,753 link opens since 13 Sep), so a row can hold a larger share than one ranked above it.

Since 13 Sep, 119,660 Priest builds were made, shared, saved or opened here and 42,920 of them have all 51 points, those today's trees no longer allow included; this list counts the 13,171 with 41 or more points that today's trees allow, since the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685.

This is a count of what people plan on this site, not a tier list. When a patch or hotfix moves, adds, removes or renames a Priest talent or changes what it needs first, the count starts again from the day after it and shows once 1,000 builds with 41 or more points are made on the new trees, as it did after the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685.
