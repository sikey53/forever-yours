How this list is counted the windows, the weights, when it starts again

Builds within a few points of each other count as one family, shown by its most used member. The rows and the spec split come from the 31,996 Warlock builds with 41 or more points planned here since the Oct 15 patch (build 70425), ranked by what people did with each family since then: a share or a save weighs 5, a link open 3, making the build 2 and a view 1. The shared, saved and opened counts run from 13 Sep, before the beta: a row's share is its family's shares, saves and link opens over every Warlock build's (6,112 shares, 12,723 saves, 120,623 link opens since 13 Sep), so a row can hold a larger share than one ranked above it.

Since 13 Sep, 127,534 Warlock builds were made, shared, saved or opened here and 47,846 of them have all 51 points, those today's trees no longer allow included; this list counts the 31,996 with 41 or more points that today's trees allow, since the Oct 15 patch (build 70425).

This is a count of what people plan on this site, not a tier list. When a patch or hotfix moves, adds, removes or renames a Warlock talent or changes what it needs first, the count starts again from the day after it and shows once 1,000 builds with 41 or more points are made on the new trees, as it did after the Oct 15 patch (build 70425).
