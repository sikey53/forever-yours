Warrior builds at level 30, the beta's cap 21 points

The beta cap rose to level 30 on 1 Oct, so 21 points. Between 1 Oct and 4 Nov 2026, 277 people-days went to 21-point Warrior builds set at level 30, and no build family has reached the floors yet (builds within four points count as one, and a family is shown once it has five or more people-days and its most-made build was made, shared or saved by at least two people on one day and three people-days in all).

No Protection 21-point build at level 30 has reached the floor yet (177 people-days in the window), though Protection leads the leveling orders, a different count from level 60: 66,162 of the 114,861 Warrior leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 (57.6%). The Protection leveling order's first 21 points are Shield Specialization 5/5, Improved Revenge 3/3, Improved Thunder Clap 3/3, Improved Bloodrage 2/2, Master of Defense 2/2, Defiance 3/3, Last Stand, Vanguard and Concussion Blow. At each step it is the path the most builds hold: 14,972 of 62,214 Protection builds hold its levels 10 to 19 and 6,292 of 26,135 hold its levels 20 to 29. How this list is counted the windows, the weights, when it starts again

Builds within a few points of each other count as one family, shown by its most used member. The rows and the spec split come from the 4,817 Warrior builds with 41 or more points planned here since the Nov 3 hotfixes to build 70755, ranked by what people did with each family since then: a share or a save weighs 5, a link open 3, making the build 2 and a view 1. The shared, saved and opened counts run from 13 Sep, before the beta: a row's share is its family's shares, saves and link opens over every Warrior build's (5,225 shares, 11,474 saves, 139,821 link opens since 13 Sep), so a row can hold a larger share than one ranked above it.

Since 13 Sep, 108,798 Warrior builds were made, shared, saved or opened here and 12,622 of them have all 51 points, those today's trees no longer allow included; this list counts the 4,817 with 41 or more points that today's trees allow, since the Nov 3 hotfixes to build 70755.

This is a count of what people plan on this site, not a tier list. When a patch or hotfix moves, adds, removes or renames a Warrior talent or changes what it needs first, the count starts again from the day after it and shows once 1,000 builds with 41 or more points are made on the new trees, as it did after the Nov 3 hotfixes to build 70755.

Which Warrior tree is the tank tree in WoW Forever?
Protection. Defiance, Protection row 3, at 3/3: Increases all threat generated in Defensive stance by an additional 20% while a shield is equipped. Vanguard, row 4: Your Charge ability is now usable while in Defensive Stance. Protection also leads 57.6% of the Warrior leveling orders recorded here, so not every Protection build is a tank build.
Is there a Warrior raid, dungeon or PvP build in WoW Forever yet?
The builds people tagged most for each, when they saved or shared them in the calculator. PvE: as a tank, Protection Warrior 7/5/39 (22 tags); as DPS, Fury Warrior 17/33/0 (24 tags). PvP: as DPS, Arms / Fury Warrior 31/20/0 (16 tags). A build is listed once it has 3 tags for the same thing; these are tags from players, not a ranking of how strong a build is.
Is this a Warrior tier list?
No. The builds are ranked by what people did with them on this site (shared, saved, opened by link, made or viewed), not by how strong they are.
Where do these Warrior build numbers come from?
