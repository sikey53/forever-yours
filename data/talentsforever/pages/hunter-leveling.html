Each order below is what most builds hold by the end of each level bracket; the share says how many of the builds that got there agree. Questions

Which spec do Hunters level as in WoW Forever?
46.5% of the 132,727 Hunter leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 lead in Survival, 31.4% in Marksmanship and 22.1% in Beast Mastery; that matches level 60, a separate count, where 40% of Hunter builds with 41 or more points lead in Survival too.
What are the first Hunter talents to take?
The leading Survival order puts its first points, levels 10 to 19, in Improved Tracking, Savage Strikes and Survivalist, more than any other ten points: 15% of the 57,324 Survival builds that reach level 19 hold exactly those ten points.
What is the Hunter build at level 20 in WoW Forever?
The leading Survival order holds 11 points at level 20: Improved Tracking 5/5, Savage Strikes 2/2, Survivalist 3/5, Surefooted 1/3. 15% of the 57,324 Survival builds that reach level 19 hold its levels 10 to 19; of those that go on, 48.1% hold levels 20 to 29.
What is the Hunter build at level 40 in WoW Forever?
The leading Survival order holds 31 points at level 40: Improved Tracking 5/5, Savage Strikes 2/2, Survivalist 3/5, Surefooted 3/3, Clever Traps 2/2, Predator's Edge 5/5, Expose Prey 2/2, Strider Kick 1/1, Resourcefulness 2/2, Lightning Reflexes 5/5, Lethal Attacks 1/5 (Marksmanship). 15% of the 57,324 Survival builds that reach level 19 hold its levels 10 to 19; of those that go on, 48.1% hold levels 20 to 29, 27.4% hold 30 to 39 and 18.8% hold 40 to 49.
Where does this Hunter leveling order come from?
From the builds people make on this site's Hunter calculator, which records the order every point was placed in. 132,727 Hunter builds with ten or more ordered points, made, shared or saved between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026, counted once per visitor per day, no identities. Each bracket is the ten points most builds hold by its last level among the builds that hold the earlier brackets; the order stops where fewer than 25 agree.
Can I follow this Hunter order in game?
Yes. The free Talents Forever addon shows a build from this site in the game's talent window and follows it as you level.
