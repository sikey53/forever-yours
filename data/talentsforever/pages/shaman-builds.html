How this list is counted the windows, the weights, when it starts again

Builds within a few points of each other count as one family, shown by its most used member. The rows and the spec split come from the 6,788 Shaman builds with 41 or more points planned here since the Nov 3 hotfixes to build 70755, ranked by what people did with each family since then: a share or a save weighs 5, a link open 3, making the build 2 and a view 1. The shared, saved and opened counts run from 13 Sep, before the beta: a row's share is its family's shares, saves and link opens over every Shaman build's (6,672 shares, 14,640 saves, 142,725 link opens since 13 Sep), so a row can hold a larger share than one ranked above it.

Since 13 Sep, 130,327 Shaman builds were made, shared, saved or opened here and 55,224 of them have all 51 points, those today's trees no longer allow included; this list counts the 6,788 with 41 or more points that today's trees allow, since the Nov 3 hotfixes to build 70755.

This is a count of what people plan on this site, not a tier list. When a patch or hotfix moves, adds, removes or renames a Shaman talent or changes what it needs first, the count starts again from the day after it and shows once 1,000 builds with 41 or more points are made on the new trees, as it did after the Nov 3 hotfixes to build 70755.
