This Rogue leveling guide is about talents: where each point goes from level 10, read from the builds people plan here; quests and zones are not part of it. 43% of the Rogue leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 lead in Subtlety, 33.9% in Combat and 23.1% in Assassination; that matches level 60, a separate count, where 51% of Rogue builds with 41 or more points lead in Subtlety too. Each order below is what most builds hold by the end of each level bracket; the share says how many of the builds that got there agree. Every order opens in the Rogue calculator, and the most popular Rogue builds page shows where they end up at 60.

The order more Rogues level in than any other. The order runs to level 39: Opportunity ×5, Improved Ambush ×3, Dirty Deeds ×2, Serrated Blades ×3, Initiative ×3, Premeditation, Ghostly Strike, Elusiveness ×2, Camouflage ×3, Hemorrhage, Preparation, Boot Knife ×5. At 2/2, Dirty Deeds: Reduces the Energy cost of your Cheap Shot and Garrote abilities by 20, and your Garrote ability no longer requires you to be behind your target. (talent text, beta client)

Questions

Which spec do Rogues level as in WoW Forever?
43% of the 53,905 Rogue leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 lead in Subtlety, 33.9% in Combat and 23.1% in Assassination; that matches level 60, a separate count, where 51% of Rogue builds with 41 or more points lead in Subtlety too.
What are the first Rogue talents to take?
The leading Subtlety order puts its first points, levels 10 to 19, in Opportunity, Improved Ambush and Dirty Deeds, more than any other ten points: 9.3% of the 17,474 Subtlety builds that reach level 19 hold exactly those ten points.
What is the Rogue build at level 20 in WoW Forever?
The leading Subtlety order holds 11 points at level 20: Opportunity 5/5, Improved Ambush 3/3, Dirty Deeds 2/2, Serrated Blades 1/3. 9.3% of the 17,474 Subtlety builds that reach level 19 hold its levels 10 to 19; of those that go on, 22.5% hold levels 20 to 29.
Where does this Rogue leveling order come from?
From the builds people make on this site's Rogue calculator, which records the order every point was placed in. 53,905 Rogue builds with ten or more ordered points, made, shared or saved between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026, counted once per visitor per day, no identities. Each bracket is the ten points most builds hold by its last level among the builds that hold the earlier brackets; the order stops where fewer than 25 agree.
Can I follow this Rogue order in game?
Yes. The free Talents Forever addon shows a build from this site in the game's talent window and follows it as you level.
