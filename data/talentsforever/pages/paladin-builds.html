Paladin builds at level 30, the beta's cap 21 points

The beta cap rose to level 30 on 1 Oct, so 21 points. Between 1 Oct and 4 Nov 2026, 22 people-days went to 21-point Paladin builds set at level 30, and no build family has reached the floors yet (builds within four points count as one, and a family is shown once it has five or more people-days and its most-made build was made, shared or saved by at least two people on one day and three people-days in all).

No Retribution 21-point build at level 30 has reached the floor yet (15 people-days in the window), though Retribution leads the leveling orders, a different count from level 60: 29,205 of the 62,450 Paladin leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 (46.8%). The Retribution leveling order's first 21 points are Benediction 5/5, Improved Judgement 2/2, Conviction 3/3, Sanctified Judgement 3/3, Seal of Command, Pursuit of Justice 2/2, Two-Handed Weapon Specialization 3/3, Sacred Arbiter and Vengeance 1/3. At each step it is the path the most builds hold: 8,352 of 24,780 Retribution builds hold its levels 10 to 19 and 4,565 of 9,357 hold its levels 20 to 29. Open it at level 30, or see the whole Retribution leveling order.

Each spec's first 21 points, as the leveling orders place them: Retribution 0/0/21, from 29,205 Retribution orders; Protection 0/21/0, from 22,510; Holy 21/0/0, from 10,735. Recorded 18 Sep to 4 Nov 2026; the Paladin leveling order has every bracket.

How this list is counted the windows, the weights, when it starts again

Builds within a few points of each other count as one family, shown by its most used member. The rows and the spec split come from the 18,620 Paladin builds with 41 or more points planned here since the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685, ranked by what people did with each family since then: a share or a save weighs 5, a link open 3, making the build 2 and a view 1. The shared, saved and opened counts run from 13 Sep, before the beta: a row's share is its family's shares, saves and link opens over every Paladin build's (6,662 shares, 13,430 saves, 118,888 link opens since 13 Sep), so a row can hold a larger share than one ranked above it.

Since 13 Sep, 117,290 Paladin builds were made, shared, saved or opened here and 13,568 of them have all 51 points, those today's trees no longer allow included; this list counts the 18,620 with 41 or more points that today's trees allow, since the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685.

This is a count of what people plan on this site, not a tier list. When a patch or hotfix moves, adds, removes or renames a Paladin talent or changes what it needs first, the count starts again from the day after it and shows once 1,000 builds with 41 or more points are made on the new trees, as it did after the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685.
