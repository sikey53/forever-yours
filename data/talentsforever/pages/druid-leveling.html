This Druid leveling guide is about talents: where each point goes from level 10, read from the builds people plan here; quests and zones are not part of it. 41% of the Druid leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 lead in Feral Combat, 33.8% in Balance and 25.2% in Restoration; that matches level 60, a separate count, where 58% of Druid builds with 41 or more points lead in Feral Combat too. Each order below is what most builds hold by the end of each level bracket; the share says how many of the builds that got there agree. Every order opens in the Druid calculator, and the most popular Druid builds page shows where they end up at 60.

Forms on the way, from the beta client's spellbook (build 70755): Cat Form at 20, Travel Form at 30 and Dire Bear Form at 40. Aquatic Form (level 16) and Bear Form (level 10) come from quests, not the trainer. Until 20, Bear Form is the only feral form. Every spell's level is on the Druid abilities page.
