Each order below is what most builds hold by the end of each level bracket; the share says how many of the builds that got there agree. Questions

Which spec do Priests level as in WoW Forever?
43.1% of the 137,799 Priest leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 lead in Discipline, 42.5% in Shadow and 14.3% in Holy; at level 60, a separate count, 46% of Priest builds with 41 or more points lead in Shadow, so many Priests level one way and respec.
What are the first Priest talents to take?
The leading Discipline order puts its first points, levels 10 to 19, in Twin Disciplines and Mental Strength, more than any other ten points: 11.8% of the 37,279 Discipline builds that reach level 19 hold exactly those ten points.
What is the Priest build at level 20 in WoW Forever?
The leading Discipline order holds 11 points at level 20: Twin Disciplines 5/5, Mental Strength 5/5, Improved Power Word: Shield 1/3. 11.8% of the 37,279 Discipline builds that reach level 19 hold its levels 10 to 19; of those that go on, 15.9% hold levels 20 to 29.
What is the Priest build at level 40 in WoW Forever?
The leading Discipline order holds 31 points at level 40: Twin Disciplines 5/5, Mental Strength 5/5, Improved Power Word: Shield 3/3, Meditation 3/3, Inner Focus 1/1, Soul Warding 1/1, Mental Agility 3/3, Renewed Hope 5/5, Penance 1/1, Divine Aegis 3/3, Power Infusion 1/1. 11.8% of the 37,279 Discipline builds that reach level 19 hold its levels 10 to 19; of those that go on, 15.9% hold levels 20 to 29, 61.1% hold 30 to 39 and 36.4% hold 40 to 49.
Where does this Priest leveling order come from?
From the builds people make on this site's Priest calculator, which records the order every point was placed in. 137,799 Priest builds with ten or more ordered points, made, shared or saved between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026, counted once per visitor per day, no identities. Each bracket is the ten points most builds hold by its last level among the builds that hold the earlier brackets; the order stops where fewer than 25 agree.
Can I follow this Priest order in game?
Yes. The free Talents Forever addon shows a build from this site in the game's talent window and follows it as you level.
