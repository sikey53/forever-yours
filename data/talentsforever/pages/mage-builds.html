Mage builds at level 30, the beta's cap 21 points

The beta cap rose to level 30 on 1 Oct, so 21 points. Between 1 Oct and 4 Nov 2026, 178 people-days went to 21-point Mage builds set at level 30, and no build family has reached the floors yet (builds within four points count as one, and a family is shown once it has five or more people-days and its most-made build was made, shared or saved by at least two people on one day and three people-days in all).

No Frost 21-point build at level 30 has reached the floor yet (102 people-days in the window), though Frost leads the leveling orders, a different count from level 60: 33,405 of the 71,372 Mage leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 (46.8%). The Frost leveling order's first 21 points are Improved Frostbolt 5/5, Ice Shards 5/5, Frostbite 3/3, Improved Frost Nova 2/2, Ice Lance, Shatter 3/3, Ice Block and Arctic Reach 1/2. At each step it is the path the most builds hold: 4,886 of 29,179 Frost builds hold its levels 10 to 19 and 908 of 11,307 hold its levels 20 to 29. Open it at level 30, or see the whole Frost leveling order.

Each spec's first 21 points, as the leveling orders place them: Frost 0/0/21, from 33,405 Frost orders; Arcane 21/0/0, from 22,224; Fire 0/21/0, from 15,743. Recorded 18 Sep to 4 Nov 2026; the Mage leveling order has every bracket.

How this list is counted the windows, the weights, when it starts again

Builds within a few points of each other count as one family, shown by its most used member. The rows and the spec split come from the 14,643 Mage builds with 41 or more points planned here since the Oct 28 hotfixes to build 70630, ranked by what people did with each family since then: a share or a save weighs 5, a link open 3, making the build 2 and a view 1. The shared, saved and opened counts run from 13 Sep, before the beta: a row's share is its family's shares, saves and link opens over every Mage build's (3,817 shares, 9,194 saves, 81,717 link opens since 13 Sep), so a row can hold a larger share than one ranked above it.

Since 13 Sep, 100,590 Mage builds were made, shared, saved or opened here and 15,449 of them have all 51 points, those today's trees no longer allow included; this list counts the 14,643 with 41 or more points that today's trees allow, since the Oct 28 hotfixes to build 70630.

This is a count of what people plan on this site, not a tier list. When a patch or hotfix moves, adds, removes or renames a Mage talent or changes what it needs first, the count starts again from the day after it and shows once 1,000 builds with 41 or more points are made on the new trees, as it did after the Oct 28 hotfixes to build 70630.

Which Mage build is for raids, PvP or dungeons?
The counter records the builds people plan, not what they plan them for, so no build here is marked raid, PvP or dungeon. The 5 most used builds above come from all 14,643 Mage builds with 41 or more points planned here since the Oct 28 hotfixes to build 70630. What the beta files say for each use: Arcane Brilliance reaches the target's party and raid (in Classic, the party), Improved Scorch raises the Fire damage you deal to the target by 3% a stack (Classic's text: the Fire damage dealt to your target) and Improved Counterspell always silences, 2 seconds at rank 1 and 4 at rank 2 (Classic's: a 50% chance of 4 seconds).
Will these Mage builds change at launch?
The count restarts when a patch changes the talents. This list counts since the Oct 28 hotfixes to build 70630 and is recounted a few times a day. The game launched on 4 November 2026.
Is this a Mage tier list?
No. The builds are ranked by what people did with them on this site (shared, saved, opened by link, made or viewed), not by how strong they are.
Where do these Mage build numbers come from?
