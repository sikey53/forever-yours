This Paladin leveling guide is about talents: where each point goes from level 10, read from the builds people plan here; quests and zones are not part of it. 46.8% of the Paladin leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 lead in Retribution, 36% in Protection and 17.2% in Holy; at level 60, a separate count, 43% of Paladin builds with 41 or more points lead in Protection, so many Paladins level one way and respec. Each order below is what most builds hold by the end of each level bracket; the share says how many of the builds that got there agree. Every order opens in the Paladin calculator, and the most popular Paladin builds page shows where they end up at 60.

Where does this Paladin leveling order come from?
From the builds people make on this site's Paladin calculator, which records the order every point was placed in. 62,450 Paladin builds with ten or more ordered points, made, shared or saved between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026, counted once per visitor per day, no identities. Each bracket is the ten points most builds hold by its last level among the builds that hold the earlier brackets; the order stops where fewer than 25 agree.
