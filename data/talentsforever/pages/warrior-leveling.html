This Warrior leveling guide is about talents: where each point goes from level 10, read from the builds people plan here; quests and zones are not part of it. 57.6% of the Warrior leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 lead in Protection, 23.9% in Fury and 18.5% in Arms; that matches level 60, a separate count, where 45% of Warrior builds with 41 or more points lead in Protection too. Each order below is what most builds hold by the end of each level bracket; the share says how many of the builds that got there agree. Every order opens in the Warrior calculator, and the most popular Warrior builds page shows where they end up at 60.

Questions

Which spec do Warriors level as in WoW Forever?
57.6% of the 114,861 Warrior leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 lead in Protection, 23.9% in Fury and 18.5% in Arms; that matches level 60, a separate count, where 45% of Warrior builds with 41 or more points lead in Protection too.
What are the first Warrior talents to take?
The leading Protection order puts its first points, levels 10 to 19, in Shield Specialization, Improved Revenge and Improved Thunder Clap, more than any other ten points: 24.1% of the 62,214 Protection builds that reach level 19 hold exactly those ten points.
What is the Warrior build at level 20 in WoW Forever?
The leading Protection order holds 11 points at level 20: Shield Specialization 5/5, Improved Revenge 3/3, Improved Thunder Clap 3/3. 24.1% of the 62,214 Protection builds that reach level 19 hold its levels 10 to 19; of those that go on, 24.1% hold levels 20 to 29.
Where does this Warrior leveling order come from?
From the builds people make on this site's Warrior calculator, which records the order every point was placed in. 114,861 Warrior builds with ten or more ordered points, made, shared or saved between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026, counted once per visitor per day, no identities. Each bracket is the ten points most builds hold by its last level among the builds that hold the earlier brackets; the order stops where fewer than 25 agree.
Can I follow this Warrior order in game?
Yes. The free Talents Forever addon shows a build from this site in the game's talent window and follows it as you level.

Builds whose finished ranks today's trees refuse (a row not open, an arrow not met) are left out.
