How this list is counted the windows, the weights, when it starts again

Builds within a few points of each other count as one family, shown by its most used member. The rows and the spec split come from the 10,160 Hunter builds with 41 or more points planned here since the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685, ranked by what people did with each family since then: a share or a save weighs 5, a link open 3, making the build 2 and a view 1. The shared, saved and opened counts run from 13 Sep, before the beta: a row's share is its family's shares, saves and link opens over every Hunter build's (4,670 shares, 9,267 saves, 97,333 link opens since 13 Sep), so a row can hold a larger share than one ranked above it.

Since 13 Sep, 89,637 Hunter builds were made, shared, saved or opened here and 47,742 of them have all 51 points, those today's trees no longer allow included; this list counts the 10,160 with 41 or more points that today's trees allow, since the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685.

This is a count of what people plan on this site, not a tier list. When a patch or hotfix moves, adds, removes or renames a Hunter talent or changes what it needs first, the count starts again from the day after it and shows once 1,000 builds with 41 or more points are made on the new trees, as it did after the Oct 30 hotfixes to build 70685.

Is this a Hunter tier list?
