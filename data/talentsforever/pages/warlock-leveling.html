Was: Hitting an enemy with Conflagrate increases all Shadow damage you deal by 2% for 20 sec, and hitting an enemy with Shadowburn increases all Fire damage you deal by 2% for 20 sec. Where does this Warlock leveling order come from?
From the builds people make on this site's Warlock calculator, which records the order every point was placed in. 170,012 Warlock builds with ten or more ordered points, made, shared or saved between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026, counted once per visitor per day, no identities. Each bracket is the ten points most builds hold by its last level among the builds that hold the earlier brackets; the order stops where fewer than 25 agree.
Can I follow this Warlock order in game?
Yes. The free Talents Forever addon shows a build from this site in the game's talent window and follows it as you level.
