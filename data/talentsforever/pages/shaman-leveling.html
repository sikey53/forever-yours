This Shaman leveling guide is about talents: where each point goes from level 10, read from the builds people plan here; quests and zones are not part of it. 58.2% of the Shaman leveling orders planned here between 18 Sep and 4 Nov 2026 lead in Enhancement, 21.8% in Restoration and 19.9% in Elemental; that matches level 60, a separate count, where 56% of Shaman builds with 41 or more points lead in Enhancement too. Each order below is what most builds hold by the end of each level bracket; the share says how many of the builds that got there agree. Every order opens in the Shaman calculator, and the most popular Shaman builds page shows where they end up at 60.
