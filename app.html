Your remaining talent order

Recalculated every time you add or remove a point, starting from what you have actually trained. This is a hybrid: cat damage first up to Shifting Power, then Primal Bite and Lacerate so bear can tank dungeons, then defense for both forms. Restoration stays free for your PvP dual spec at 40.

Legacy perks

Legacy points are earned once on your account from 65 challenges, and every character spends up to 16 of them on its own perks. Your first point usually comes at level 25 on any class.

How to earn Legacy points

Classes give 27 points: reach level 25, 45 and 60 on each class. That is 3 for your druid.

Professions give 18: reach 150, 225 and 300 in each non-gathering profession.

PvP gives 12: honor ranks 3, 7, 10, 13 and 14, Exalted with each battleground faction, and three Field of Honor weekly quests.

Dungeons give 3: the final boss of every dungeon from 15 to 25, from 26 to 45, and from 46 to 60. Raids give 3: Onyxia, all of Hyjal Summit and all of Barrow Deeps.

Adventure gives 2: explore the whole world map on one character, and finish the Lord Valthalak questline.

From Blizzard's Legacy announcement and Talents Forever. Blizzard has not said how to respec perks.

How to fight

Each line is one press, top to bottom. The number after a spell is the level you learn it, so skip a line until you have it.

Rotations

Cat · questing and dungeons
  1. Before you pull: ⇧Q Mark of the Wild and ⇧F Thorns on yourself.
  2. Open from V Prowl with E Ravage from behind (32) or Q Pounce to stun (36). Before 32, open from Prowl with 1 Rake. Without Prowl, pull with F Faerie Fire.
  3. Build combo points with E Shred when you are behind, Q Claw otherwise.
  4. 1 Rake once on any mob that will live a while (24). Skip it on mobs about to die.
  5. At 4 or 5 combo points: 2 Rip for long fights, 3 Ferocious Bite when you have 70 energy or more, or the mob is low (24).
  6. R Shifting Power whenever energy drops under 50 (40).
  7. Two or more mobs: cat has no area attack. Root the extra one with R Entangling Roots in caster first, or go ⇧1 bear and Swipe.
  8. After the fight: Middle heal and drink. Furor keeps your cat energy while you are out.
Bear · packs and tanking
  1. Pull with F Faerie Fire from range, or 5 Feral Charge to close in. The charge also interrupts.
  2. R Enrage for rage at the start, then 1 Demoralizing Roar on the pack.
  3. 4 Primal Bite every time it is ready (44).
  4. One big target: 2 Lacerate up to 5 stacks and keep them rolling (45), Q Maul with spare rage.
  5. Two or more mobs: E Swipe, with Primal Bite on whatever is loose.
  6. 3 Growl a mob that leaves you. Click Challenging Roar if the whole pack runs at the healer.
  7. X Bash a caster mid cast. It fires Enrage too, so you always have the rage.
  8. Getting low: Mouse 4 potion, V Frenzied Regeneration (36), C Barkskin (44).
Caster · saving the group
  1. Middle drops your form and starts a heal in one press.
  2. E Rejuvenation on the tank early. It is cheap and keeps working while you do other things.
  3. 3 Regrowth when someone drops fast, Q Healing Touch when they are low and you are safe to cast.
  4. Hover a party frame and press 4 for poison or 5 for a curse.
  5. ⇧E Tranquility when the whole group is low, and do not move (30).
  6. V Innervate on yourself when mana runs out (40). ⇧R Rebirth if someone dies mid fight.

When a player attacks you

Find the card that matches and follow it. If you panic, use the first card: it works against everyone.

Any fight going badly
  1. Mouse 4 potion. It works in any form, so it costs you nothing.
  2. ⇧3 Nature's Grasp, so the next melee hit roots them.
  3. ⇧X War Stomp to stun everyone around you for a moment.
  4. Use that moment: ⇧Middle instant Rejuvenation, then ⇧1 bear for armor, or Mouse 5 to run in cat.
  5. From 44, press C Barkskin before a cast heal so hits cannot push it back.
A rogue opens on you
  1. You cannot act while stunned or sapped. Wait for it to end and do not mash keys.
  2. F Faerie Fire on the rogue first: it cannot go back into stealth while it lasts.
  3. ⇧3 Nature's Grasp, then ⇧X War Stomp when it is next to you.
  4. Healthy: fight it in cat with bleeds. Low: ⇧1 bear, Mouse 4 potion, V Frenzied Regeneration.
A warrior or melee keeps slowing you
  1. Shifting removes roots and slows. Press ⇧2 twice in cat, or ⇧1 twice in bear. It costs mana each time.
  2. ⇧3 Nature's Grasp so their next hit roots them, then walk out of range.
  3. Mouse 5 Dash away, or Z Travel Form outdoors (30).
  4. Never stand still to cast Regrowth next to a warrior. Use ⇧Middle instant Rejuvenation while you run.
A mage
  1. Stay in cat or bear. An animal form cannot be turned into a sheep.
  2. Frost Nova roots you: shift out of it with ⇧2 twice.
  3. Close in with 5 Feral Charge, the cat leap or the bear charge, which also interrupts.
  4. Stop big casts with ⇧X War Stomp, or X Bash in bear.
A warlock or priest, fears and casters
  1. Druids have no way to break a fear, and War Stomp does not work while you are feared. So stop the fear before it lands.
  2. Get into melee fast with 5 Feral Charge, then interrupt casts with ⇧X War Stomp or X Bash.
  3. When a fear ends, ⇧Middle instant Rejuvenation first, then go back on them.
  4. A PvP trinket that breaks fear, if Forever gives you one, goes on ⇧C.
A hunter or an enemy druid
  1. Put the hunter's pet to sleep with ⇧V Hibernate from caster form, then fight the hunter up close where it is weakest.
  2. Hibernate also works on an enemy druid in cat or bear form.
  3. Shift out of Wing Clip and other slows with ⇧2 twice, then 5 Feral Charge back in.
Two or more players
  1. Do not fight. ⇧3 Nature's Grasp and ⇧X War Stomp to buy a few seconds.
  2. Mouse 5 Dash away in cat, and shift out of every slow.
  3. Mouse 4 potion and ⇧Middle instant Rejuvenation while running. Never stop to cast.
  4. Run toward guards, friends or a doorway that breaks their line of sight.

The bright part of each bar is the window worth questing in. Leave about 2 levels before the zone's top: past that, most of its quests sit 5 or more levels below you and give much less experience.

Dungeons
Experience rules in Forever

Dungeon kills give 50% more experience since the November 8 hotfix. Dungeon quests give 50% less bonus experience than in the beta, so a dungeon is worth it for the kills.

In a group, kill experience depends only on your own level. But if anyone in the group is far above the enemy, nobody gets experience from it.

Quest experience in Classic drops once a quest is 6 levels below you: 80%, then 60%, 40%, 20% and finally 10%. Blizzard has not said whether Forever changed this, so the leave-by levels follow that rule.

Quest counts per zone are not published yet, except Riverglades with over 150. The daily check adds them when guides do.

Sources: Outputlag leveling guide, TopBuilds Horde route. Ranges for the original dungeons are their Classic values.

Play notes

Forever is built to keep you in one form. Going into bear wipes the energy you saved, so in cat interrupt with War Stomp on Shift X first and keep the X route into bear as the backup.

Every shapeshift starts a global cooldown, about a second. /cancelform never does, which is why the middle mouse heal starts instantly.

Fire Shifting Power when your energy drops under 50 so none of the 40 is wasted. Your mana bar is hidden in forms, so turn on Options, Combat, Personal Resource Display.

Enrage gives 10 rage instantly in Forever, which is exactly what Bash costs. Furor also gives 10 rage when you enter bear.

Barkskin works in any form from level 44, so C becomes the same defensive everywhere. Faerie Fire is free in cat and bear with a 6 second cooldown.

Potions, bandages and most objects work without leaving form. Tranquility, Rebirth, Innervate and the buffs still need caster form: tap middle mouse first.

Setting it up in game
  1. In Options, Keybindings, clear any default that sits on a key your bars use below.
  2. Bind Action Bar 1, your main bar at the bottom of the screen, button 1 to 12 to the keys in the order shown. That bar swaps by itself with each form.
  3. Bind the stance bar icons to the keys shown on the stance bar. Bear is the first icon and cat usually the third, counted from the left.
  4. Turn on Action Bars 2, 3 and 4 in the game's action bar settings. Bind them in the order shown, pressing the mouse buttons themselves for Action Bar 4. Leave the side buttons unassigned in your mouse software.
  5. Go into cat, bear and caster one at a time and drag that form's spells onto the main bar. Make the macros with /macro.
