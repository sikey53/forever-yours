Legacy perks

Legacy points are earned once on your account from 65 challenges, and every character spends up to 16 of them on its own perks. Your first point usually comes at level 25 on any class.

How to earn Legacy points

Classes give 27 points: reach level 25, 45 and 60 on each class. That is 3 for your druid.

Professions give 18: reach 150, 225 and 300 in each non-gathering profession.

PvP gives 12: honor ranks 3, 7, 10, 13 and 14, Exalted with each battleground faction, and three Field of Honor weekly quests.

Dungeons give 3: the final boss of every dungeon from 15 to 25, from 26 to 45, and from 46 to 60. Raids give 3: Onyxia, all of Hyjal Summit and all of Barrow Deeps.

Adventure gives 2: explore the whole world map on one character, and finish the Lord Valthalak questline.

From Blizzard's Legacy announcement and Talents Forever. Blizzard has not said how to respec perks.

Questing zones

The bright part of each bar is the window worth questing in. Leave about 2 levels before the zone's top: past that, most of its quests sit 5 or more levels below you and give much less experience.

Dungeons
Experience rules in Forever

Dungeon kills give 50% more experience since the November 8 hotfix. Dungeon quests give 50% less bonus experience than in the beta, so a dungeon is worth it for the kills.

In a group, kill experience depends only on your own level. But if anyone in the group is far above the enemy, nobody gets experience from it.

Quest experience in Classic drops once a quest is 6 levels below you: 80%, then 60%, 40%, 20% and finally 10%. Blizzard has not said whether Forever changed this, so the leave-by levels follow that rule.

Quest counts per zone are not published yet, except Riverglades with over 150. The daily check adds them when guides do.

Sources: Outputlag leveling guide, TopBuilds Horde route. Ranges for the original dungeons are their Classic values.

Setting it up in game
  1. In Options, Keybindings, clear any default that sits on a key your bars use below.
  2. Bind Action Bar 1, your main bar at the bottom of the screen, button 1 to 12 to the keys in the order shown. That bar swaps by itself with each form.
  3. Bind the stance bar icons to the keys shown on the stance bar. Bear is the first icon and cat usually the third, counted from the left.
  4. Turn on Action Bars 2, 3 and 4 in the game's action bar settings. Bind them in the order shown, pressing the mouse buttons themselves for Action Bar 4. Leave the side buttons unassigned in your mouse software.
  5. Go into cat, bear and caster one at a time and drag that form's spells onto the main bar. Make the macros with /macro.
